The ecosystem

How Ajo Mercy works.

A simple process. A person at every important step. No stage skips the one before it.

process diagram / illustration placeholder
01

A business steps forward

The owner tells us what they run, what they're building, and what's standing in the way. Applying is free and takes one sitting.

02

A person verifies it

The review team reads every application and opens every document by hand. Nothing goes live automatically.

03

People discover it

Verified businesses appear publicly with their own story. Approved supporters can choose the ones they want to back.

04

A choice is made

Choosing is not confirming. The business hears nothing definite yet — it's under consideration.

05

The team confirms

A person checks with both sides before the business is told anything is real. Only then can support be confirmed.

06

The story moves forward

Confirmed support can be announced publicly, and the business's story continues on its profile.

About Ajo Mercy

An argument about Ajo went around Nigeria and turned into something else. People started asking a practical question: if I wanted to help a small business right now, how would I know which one was real?

Ajo Mercy is the part that was missing. Business owners apply and are verified by a person. Supporters and brands are verified too. Then they choose, we confirm, and we follow the support through to the point where it arrives.

Selection is not confirmation

When a supporter selects a business, that business is told it is under consideration, in those words. Nobody is congratulated until a person on our team has confirmed the support is real.

We do not touch the money

Ajo Mercy holds no funds, operates no wallet and transfers nothing. Support is arranged directly between the supporter and the business, with our team coordinating and following up.

Who runs it

Woli Arole

Super Admin. He and the Ajo Mercy team review applications, approve businesses and confirm every recipient.

Our commitments

  • Applying is free and always will be.
  • We will never ask anyone for a fee.
  • We do not publish selection counts.
  • Registering does not guarantee support.

For brands

You have a budget and a list of people asking for it. What you don't have is a way to tell which are real. That's the part we've already done.
