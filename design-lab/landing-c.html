§1

Method

Three stages. No stage is automatic.
  1. Stage 01

    A business applies

    Owner, business, documents, and the story behind it.

  2. Stage 02

    A person reviews it

    Woli Arole and the Ajo Mercy team check every application by hand. Nothing goes live automatically.

  3. Stage 03

    Supporters choose

    Individuals and brands pick who they want to back. We confirm before anyone is told they have been supported.

§3

Origin

An argument about Ajo turned into a queue of people asking the same question: who actually needs the money, and how do I know they are real?

Brands started calling. Individuals started offering. Business owners started sending DMs. None of it had a system behind it, so most of it went nowhere.

Ajo Mercy is the system. Applications get read. Documents get checked. Businesses that pass get a profile with their real story on it. Supporters and brands choose from those, and no one is told they have been supported until the team has confirmed it.

The review team is named on the About page. Every decision is written to an audit log with the reviewer attached.

Figures on the Impact page are counted from the database. Where a figure has not happened yet, the page shows a dash rather than a zero dressed up as progress.

§4

Safeguards

Verification is the whole product.
4.1

Reviewed by a person, not a filter

Woli Arole is the Super Admin. He and the review team see every application, every document and every photograph before a profile goes live.

4.2

Selection is not confirmation

When a supporter picks a business, that business is told it is under consideration. Only after the team confirms does anyone hear the word confirmed.

4.3

We never touch the money

Ajo Mercy does not hold, escrow or transfer funds. Support is arranged directly, and we stay in the loop to make sure it lands.
